Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 20, 2026

This policy explains how Oppa Language ("the app") handles your information.

1. Accounts and personal information

The app has no account sign-up. It does not collect your name, email address, phone number, or other personal information.

2. Audio and the microphone

Audio recorded with the microphone is used only to score your pronunciation. On supported devices, speech recognition runs on the device. The developer does not receive your recordings or what you said.

3. Usage analytics

To improve the app and investigate problems, the app may send the following usage data to the analytics service TelemetryDeck:

  • A hash of a randomly generated device identifier
  • The app version, OS and device model, and language and region settings
  • Usage events such as the courses or teachers you pick, screens viewed, lessons completed, and purchase outcomes

The app does not send what you said, speech recognition results, text you type, your name, your email address, or advertising identifiers.

4. How we use and keep this data

Analytics data is used only to improve the app and investigate problems. It is kept only as long as needed for those purposes and deleted when no longer needed. We require service providers, including TelemetryDeck, to protect it to the same standard.

5. Data on your device

Your progress and settings are stored on your device. Deleting the app deletes this data.

6. Purchases

Subscriptions are processed by Apple. The developer never receives your credit card or other payment details.

7. Sharing with third parties

We do not sell or share your information with third parties, except where required by law.

8. Contact

To ask about this policy or to request access to or deletion of your data, email rebirth.sakuraya@gmail.com.

9. Changes

If this policy changes, we will post the update on this page.
