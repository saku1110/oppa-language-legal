Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 10, 2026

This policy explains how Oppa Language ("the app") handles your information.

1. Accounts and personal information

The app has no account sign-up. It does not collect your name, email address, phone number, or other personal information.

2. Audio and the microphone

Audio recorded with the microphone is used only to score your pronunciation. On supported devices, speech recognition runs on the device. The developer does not receive your recordings or what you said.

3. Usage analytics

The app does not currently send usage analytics. If we add an analytics service, we will update this page and describe what is sent.

4. Purchases

Subscriptions are processed by Apple. The developer never receives your credit card or other payment details.

To check your purchase status and unlock paid features, the app uses the subscription management service RevenueCat. The following is sent to RevenueCat:

  • An anonymous ID randomly generated by the app
  • Purchase history, such as the product you bought, the purchase date, and renewal or cancellation status
  • The app version, OS type, and language, region, and currency settings

This information is used only to confirm purchases, provide paid features, and total up sales. It is not linked to your name or email address.

5. How we use and keep this data

The information above is used only for the purposes described. It is kept only as long as needed for those purposes and deleted when no longer needed. We require service providers, including RevenueCat, to protect it to the same standard.

6. Your learning data

Your progress and settings are stored on your device. If you use iCloud, they are also stored in your own iCloud so you can continue on another device signed in to the same Apple Account. The developer does not receive this data.

Deleting the app deletes the data on your device. Data stored in iCloud can be deleted from iCloud storage management in your device's Settings.

7. Sharing with third parties

We do not sell or share your information with third parties, except where required by law and for the service providers described above.

8. Contact

To ask about this policy or to request access to or deletion of your data, email rebirth.sakuraya@gmail.com.

9. Changes

If this policy changes, we will post the update on this page.
