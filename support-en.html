Canceling a subscription

  1. Open the Settings app on your device.
  2. Tap your name.
  3. Tap Subscriptions, choose Oppa Language, and cancel.

If you cancel at least 24 hours before your next renewal date, you won't be charged again. You keep Premium until the end of the period you've already paid for.

Refunds

Refunds follow Apple's policies. You can request one on Apple's refund request page.

FAQ

  • I paid but Premium isn't active. Open Settings in the app and tap Restore purchases. If that doesn't fix it, email us at the address above.
  • Pronunciation scoring doesn't work. In your device's Settings, check that Oppa Language has access to the microphone and speech recognition.
  • The daily call doesn't arrive. Check that notifications are allowed for Oppa Language. On days you've already practiced, the call won't ring.
